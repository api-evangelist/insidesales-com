Playbooks Solution for Call Compliance Adhere to Call Compliance Regulations with Playbooks Making outbound calls to customers in a wide variety of locations while observing compliance regulations can be a complicated problem. Playbooks has call settings for outbound calls that help prevent compliance violations. Block calls to certain states based on regional issues such as state of emergencies or restrict calling to locations with local holidays.
